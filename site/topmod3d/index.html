TopMod 3Dv3.0

TopMod, the topological mesh modeler, now in the browser and on iPhone and iPad. Version 3.0 is reformatted for the web and touch, with more than 80 new tools for generating, deforming, analysing and fabricating meshes.

    New in v3.0

    TopMod's core is Ergun Akleman's: the DLFL kernel and its insert and delete edge, extrusion, handle, crust, wireframe and remeshing tools. Version 3.0 keeps all of that and adds the tools below.

    Try the new tools ↗
    iPhone + iPad

    TopMod 3D is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, free: Ergun Akleman's TopMod and the new v3.0 tools, built for touch.

    Download on theApp Store
    Runs anywhereBrowser and iPhone and iPad, no install. Mouse, touch, and webcam hand tracking. Demo mode, a command bar, and a gumball to move, rotate and scale.
    Layers + modifiersLayers, and a non-destructive modifier stack you can edit, reorder and bake.
    New primitivesCuboctahedron, torus, cylinder, Möbius strip, spiral coil, polygon prism, and 3D text.
    ModelingArray (rect, honeycomb, polar, sphere, Fibonacci), mirror, bridge, pinch extrude, greeble, offset shell, cut by plane, orient, heal, fill holes, flip, simplify, quad remesh.
    GeneratorsMetaballs, gyroid, TPMS blocks, Schoen hybrid, Rössler attractor, cymatics, pseudospherical surfaces, woven tubes, chain mail, Voronoi, tree, GEM matrix, 2D and 3D mazes, breeze blocks, lattice cage morph.
    DeformersTwist, bend, taper, bulge, spherify, and noise displacement, all non-destructive.
    BooleansUnion, difference and intersect.
    AnalysisMesh diagnose, curvature, section plane, interference, wall thickness, measure, overhang, edge lengths, seams, X-ray.
    3D printingPrinter bed and build volume preview, tree supports, split, drill, voxelize, flatten.
    FabricationPapercraft unfold with glue tabs, plywood layers with dowel registration, flat plywood faces, laser cut with finger joints, waffle egg-crate slicing, nest optimizer.
    ExportOBJ, STL and STEP for 3D, DXF and PDF for cutting.
    LooksGalaxy, Matrix, water, moonwater, carbon fiber, red carpet, flower, laser, patent halftone, 3D glasses, techno and gold display modes.
    Hands

    Model with your hands. Switch on ✋ Hands and TopMod reads your webcam with MediaPipe hand tracking, so pinch gestures drive the view. Tracking runs in the browser.

    OrbitPinch with your right hand and drag.
    ZoomPinch with both hands and pull them apart or together.
    PanPinch with both hands and move them together.

    Credits + files

    TopMod was created by Dr. Ergun Akleman and his students at Texas A&M University: the topological mesh modeling method, the DLFL kernel and the original application.

    TopMod 3D v3.0 is John Briscella's (AMAXiMAL) reformatting of TopMod for the browser and iPhone and iPad, in collaboration with Ergun Akleman, with the new tools listed under New in v3.
